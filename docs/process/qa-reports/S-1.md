## Manual checks for the PO (after merge, on https://sabeekhann.github.io/Web-Calculator/)
Do these in each of **Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari** (current versions):
1. **Page loads:** open the link. You see "Tip & Bill Splitter", the boxes Bill, Tip % (already showing 0) and People, and a Calculate button.
2. **Normal split:** click Bill and type `100.00`, click People and type `3`, click Calculate. You see "Total: 100.00", then "Person 1: 33.34", "Person 2: 33.33", "Person 3: 33.33".
3. **Change and recalculate:** click People, change 3 to `4`. The result disappears straight away. Click Calculate. You see "Total: 100.00" and four lines of 25.00.
4. **Error shows and clears:** change Bill to `12,50` (with a comma), People to `2`, click Calculate. Red text under Bill reads "Use a dot for decimals and no commas, for example 12.50." Change the comma to a dot (`12.50`). The red text disappears while you type. Click Calculate. You see "Total: 12.50", 6.25 and 6.25.
5. **Keyboard only:** reload the page and don't use the mouse. Press Tab: a clear blue ring goes round Bill. Type `100.00`. Tab (Tip %), Tab (People), type `3`, then press Enter. The result appears. Also check that Tab reaches Calculate and that it shows the blue ring.
6. **375px:** on a phone, or with a very narrow window (Chrome/Edge: F12 → device toolbar → width 375), repeat step 2. Everything is readable and there is no sideways scrolling.
7. **No console errors:** open developer tools (Chrome/Edge/Firefox: F12 → Console; Safari: Settings → Advanced → "Show features for web developers", then Develop → Show JavaScript Console). After steps 1–6 there are no red errors.
