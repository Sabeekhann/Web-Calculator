## Manual checks for the PO (real Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari)
Open the app from the Pages URL or a local run. Do each step in every browser.
1. Keep the pass mark at 70. Type 15.5 in Marks earned and 23 in Total. Expected: "67.3%", "Fail", and the line "0.6 marks short of the pass mark" with a small downward marker.
2. Change Marks earned to 17.5. Expected: "76.0%", "Pass", "1.4 marks above the pass mark".
3. Enter 17.5 of 25. Expected: "Exactly on the pass mark".
4. Enter 13 of 20, then 15 of 20. Expected: "1 mark short of the pass mark", then "1 mark above the pass mark" (singular "mark").
5. Set the pass mark to 69.9 with a total of 23. Enter 16.07, then 16.08. Expected: "69.8%" Fail "0.01 marks short …", then "69.9%" Pass "Less than 0.01 marks above the pass mark".
6. Set the total to 1000000 and the pass mark to 70, and enter 0.01. Expected: "699,999.99 marks short of the pass mark", with commas.
7. Delete the pass mark. Expected: the gap line disappears and "Enter a pass mark …" shows. Type 70 again: the line returns, and the Next learner button does not move.
8. Copy "17.5" from another app and paste it into Marks earned (with total 23). Expected: "1.4 marks above the pass mark".
9. Switch the device to dark mode. Expected: the gap line and its marker are readable.
10. On a phone (Safari or Chrome), repeat step 1. Expected: the line fits the card or wraps neatly, with no sideways scrolling.
11. Screen reader (VoiceOver or NVDA), optional: after step 2, pause. Expected: "76.0%, Pass, 1.4 marks above the pass mark" is read once.
